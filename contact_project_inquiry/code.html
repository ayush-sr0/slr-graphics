TRANSPARENCY IN PRACTICE

Frequently Clarified Logistics

Everything you need to understand our collaborative engagement model.

How quickly can we officially commence production? expand_more
Most projects kick off within 10 to 14 business days post-agreement and deposit. If your requirements call for an expedited sprint, select our "< 2 Weeks" horizon in the form above and our fast-track team will evaluate schedule alignment.
What does our financial arrangement look like? expand_more
Standard engagements are structured on a milestone cadence: 50% upon project kickoff, 25% at midpoint milestone validation, and 25% preceding ultimate deployment and IP handover. Long-term retainers operate on a monthly pre-bill model.
How do you manage cross-timezone collaboration? expand_more
With our principal studio hub based in New Delhi (IST) and seamless remote collaboration tools, our studio ensures prompt communication, structured review cycles, and dedicated support for all our clients worldwide.
Will we receive complete IP ownership of all design files? expand_more
Yes. Upon final settlement, all intellectual property rights, master production files (Figma, Blender 3D, After Effects, SVG, vector packages), and production code repositories transfer wholly and unconditionally to your organization.
