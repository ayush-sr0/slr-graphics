## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a futuristic, luxury digital studio aesthetic engineered for world-class creative production, immersive design artifacts, and high-caliber digital experiences. It merges cutting-edge spatial computing influences with refined editorial precision. 

The visual personality hinges on deep spatial depth, glowing glassmorphic surfaces, and luminous chromatic highlights. The atmosphere balances cinematic darkness with high-energy radiant violet accents, projecting prestige, visionary technology, and bespoke craftsmanship. Interfaces should feel less like software and more like an elevated, responsive digital gallery or luminescent creative console.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in a nocturnal, atmospheric foundation illuminated by high-voltage violet gradients and delicate luminescent borders.

- **Background Canvas (`#1B1035`)**: The core foundational dark canvas, providing a rich, optical-density deep violet-black void.
- **Primary Radiant Accent (`#A855F7`)**: Used for luminous highlights, active indicators, interactive states, key callouts, and gradient endpoints.
- **Secondary Structural Violet (`#7C3AED`)**: The driving anchor for interactive buttons, vibrant backdrops, and dimensional linear fills.
- **Tertiary Soft Violet (`#DDD6FE`)**: High-legibility, tinted sub-headers, tags, subtle icon fills, and low-contrast UI accents.
- **Text & Accompanying Neutrals**:
  - `Pure White (#FFFFFF)`: Primary headers, critical actionable labels, and high-impact emphasis.
  - `Muted Slate Violet (#94A3B8)`: Informational secondary copy, captions, and unfocused states.
  - `Atmospheric White (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`)`: Body copy providing optimal readability without overpowering glowing UI elements.
  - `Glass Borders (`rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.2)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`)`: Ultra-fine micro-stroke boundaries simulating precision-milled frosted glass.

## Typography

The typographical pairing expresses tech-forward elegance and immaculate editorial restraint:

- **Headlines & Display (`Sora`)**: Geometric, wide, and futuristic. With distinct circular curves and balanced apexes, Sora commands dramatic presence in hero areas, case study titles, and dynamic studio headlines. It is paired with tight negative tracking to enforce cohesion.
- **Body & Functional UI (`Hanken Grotesk`)**: An exceptionally crisp, contemporary grotesque typeface built for readability at all display scales. Its neutral demeanor tempers the expressive energy of Sora, ensuring project narratives, metrics, metadata, and body narratives remain fluid and clear.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a structured, expansive 12-column fluid grid system that flexes seamlessly across diverse displays:

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns with `gutter-desktop` (2rem / 32px) and expansive `margin-desktop` (4rem / 64px) to preserve high-luxury architectural negative space.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns with 1.5rem (24px) gutters and 2rem (32px) exterior margins.
- **Mobile (Below 768px)**: 4 columns with 1rem (16px) gutters and 1.25rem (20px) margins.

Vertical rhythm relies on generous section pacing (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) to let showcase imagery, immersive mockups, and glass containers breathe without visual clutter. Component padding maintains a consistent 8pt incremental scale.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through an optical fusion of layered glassmorphism, directional ambient glows, and illuminated micro-strokes:

- **Ground Plane (Base)**: `#1B1035` textured with subtle, deep radial gradient shifts toward `#25144D`.
- **Level 1 (Surface Cards & Panels)**: Translucent surface using `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, blended with a `16px` to `24px` backdrop-filter blur. Outlined by an ultra-thin `1px` border of `rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.2)` fading into `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` along the light axis.
- **Level 2 (Active States & Modals)**: `rgba(27, 16, 53, 0.85)` surface with `32px` backdrop blur, surrounded by an elevated ambient glow: `box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5), 0 0 24px -2px rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.25)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Controls, Cursors & Indicators)**: Saturated linear fills (`linear-gradient(135deg, #A855F7 0%, #7C3AED 100%)`) with an intensified corona effect: `0 8px 24px 0 rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.4)`.

## Shapes

With a roundedness value of 3, the design system adopts a continuous, modern pill-shaped curvature philosophy. 

- **Pill Forms (`rounded-full`)**: Applied to all primary CTA buttons, navigation pills, status tags, and chips to deliver an organic, tactile, and future-forward studio feel.
- **Container Curvature (`rounded-lg: 2rem` to `rounded-xl: 3rem`)**: Feature cards, modular media panels, and modal containers use generous corner sweeps, softening the tech-heavy aura and providing an unmistakably custom, architectural posture.
- **Input Fields & Small Surfaces (`rounded: 1rem`)**: Standard interactive inputs and list surfaces share this smooth continuity to prevent abrupt visual edges across complex layouts.

## Components

- **Buttons**:
  - *Primary*: Vibrant linear gradient from `#A855F7` to `#7C3AED`, white bold text (`label-md`), full pill shape, finished with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 4px 20px rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.35)`). On hover, scale smoothly (1.02) and boost shadow intensity.
  - *Secondary / Ghost*: Frosted glass fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`), `1px` border of `rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.3)`, text in `Pure White`. Hover transitions to solid glass fill with an electric violet micro-glow.
- **Cards**:
  - Built with backdrop blur (`20px`), background tint (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`), continuous corner rounding (`2rem`), and a distinct gradient stroke (`1px` linear gradient from `rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.4)` top-left to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` bottom-right). Hover lifts the card slightly with an intensified outer violet aura.
- **Chips & Badges**:
  - Ultra-compact pill silhouettes with upper-case tracking (`label-sm`). Subdued violet background (`rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.15)`), border `rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.35)`, text color `#DDD6FE`.
- **Input Fields**:
  - Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`, `1rem` corner rounding, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. Placeholder text in `#94A3B8`. On focus, border transitions smoothly to `#A855F7` coupled with an inner and outer focus ring glow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.2)`).
- **Lists & Selection Controls**:
  - Checkboxes and radio buttons feature custom pill-like soft radios with gradient borders. When active, they fill with `#A855F7` and deliver a subtle radial tick mark. List rows employ dividing lines rendered via hairline alpha gradients (`linear-gradient(90deg, transparent, rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.2), transparent)`).
- **Studio Portfolio Showcase Component**:
  - Extended full-width card with an embedded dynamic media canvas, overlaid with contextual floating meta-pills and magnetic cursor interaction points.
